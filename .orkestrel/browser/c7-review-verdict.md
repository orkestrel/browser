Rulings (the fix brief `tmp/units/c7-fix-brief.md`):

- F3 (choice 2): a navigation that outran the read leaves the submission unknown, so the clause stays `and pressed Enter` with the destination view and the C6 statuses unchanged; the expectations and the guide follow.
- F4 (O1): the read record's booleans are validated; an off-shape record is unknown, never "no form received the submission".
- F5 (O2): the three matrices move to `tests/setup.ts` with their proofs, the registrations and assertions staying in the test files.
- Choices 1, 3, and 4 (stated in the lane's hand-back, not the claims file): the `submitted` and `implicit` fields stand under F2 and F4; the unit click cases on the fixture's link with a scripted observer stand because the service `/shop` case covers a real submit button; the fixture built from the ledger's description of the v6 checkout stands as a bounded substitution for the transcripts the lane could not read.
